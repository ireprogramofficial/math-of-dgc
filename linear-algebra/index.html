The DGC problem

$$\begin{aligned} \min_{u \in \mathbb{R}^{M \times (K-1)}} \quad & \bigl\| \, x[K] - \textcolor{#1a5fb4}{x_{\text{target}}} \, \bigr\|_2 \\ \text{s.t.} \quad & x[k+1] = \textcolor{#1a5fb4}{A[k]}\, x[k] + \textcolor{#e8501a}{B_R}\, u[k], \quad k = 1, \dots, K-1 \\ & u[k] \ge 0 \\ & x[1] = \textcolor{#1a5fb4}{x_{\text{initial}}}, \quad x[K] \text{ free} \end{aligned}$$

Blue terms come from the time-series data, orange from the regulation network. Indexing is 1-based: \(K = 48\) time points, so 47 steps and 47 matrices \(A[1], \dots, A[47]\).
