From measurements to transition matrices

What one \(A_k\) does

$$A_k = I + \frac{(\tilde e_{k+1} - \tilde e_k)\,\tilde e_k^{\mathsf T}}{\tilde e_k^{\mathsf T}\tilde e_k}$$

Exact — \(\tilde e_k\) lands on \(\tilde e_{k+1}\). The step in the data is reproduced, not approximated.

Untouched — every direction orthogonal to \(\tilde e_k\) is left alone green.

One stretch — the only eigenvalue that isn't 1 is \(\lambda\), along \(\tilde e_{k+1} - \tilde e_k\) purple.

Closest to \(I\) — no other matrix that sends \(\tilde e_k\) to \(\tilde e_{k+1}\) is nearer the identity.

In the real model the same picture holds in the plane of \(\tilde e_k\) and \(\tilde e_{k+1}\); the other 2,094 directions orthogonal to \(\tilde e_k\) stay fixed.
